A one-image summary of world news for Wednesday 18 November 2026. `draw.py` (Python + PIL, DejaVu Sans) draws
everything from code, with no photographs, and writes `entry.png` (1024x1024). `main.ts` (Node 22, standard
library only) copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).

## Stories featured
1. APEC summit opens in Shenzhen; Xi greets Trump on arrival.
2. Iran and the US trade strikes in the Gulf; Kuwait and Bahrain bases targeted.
3. Putin "not ready" for serious Ukraine peace talks, per New York Times sources.
4. Mary Peltola wins the Alaska Senate seat, giving Democrats a 56-44 Senate majority.
5. US officially loses measles elimination status (acknowledged by HHS Secretary RFK Jr.).
